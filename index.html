Alexa Voice Control

The Amazon Echo Dot in the living room responds to your voice. Just say "Alexa," followed by your command. No app needed — it works instantly.

"Alexa, Volume Up / Down the TV" Increases or decreases TV sound by 10 points
"Alexa, Turn On / Off Living Room Lights" Turns on or off all lights in the living room
"Alexa, How is the Weather?" Alexa tells you the current weather forecast for Burgas
"Alexa, Set a timer for 10 minutes" Useful for cooking, workouts, or any timed activity

✨ Terrace Curtain Light Scenes

"Alexa, change the scene on the curtain lights to Delphine" Flowing blue & white wave effect across the LED curtain
"Alexa, change the scene on the curtain lights to Traffic" Dynamic multicolour chasing pattern — vivid party atmosphere
"Alexa, change the scene on the curtain lights to Proposal" Romantic warm-glow scene with slow colour transitions

Parking & Check-in Instructions

Step-by-step Arrival Guide

1

Building Entrance — The entrance is located right next to Pulse Vital Fitness. Look for the lockbox directly in front of the building entrance door.

2

Lockbox — Enter the PIN code provided in your booking message to open the lockbox. Inside you will find your key set.

3

Garage Entry — Use the remote control from the key set to open the garage door and drive in.

4

Parking Space 31 — Your designated spot is No. 31, immediately on the right after entering the garage.

5

Elevator Access — After parking, continue straight until you see the yellow parking area. The entrance door to the elevators is just behind it. Go through the door, then turn left to find the elevators.

6

12th Floor — Take the elevator to floor 12. Use the green tag to tap next to the camera by the entrance door. Use the orange tag on the elevator reader first, then press floor 12.
